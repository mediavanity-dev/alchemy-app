Sing the tune in your head. Find its key.

Alchemy listens to the melody you sing, finds the key it fits and the chords that go with it. A composition tool for songwriters and singers: from a hummed idea to a song you can play.

From a hum to a harmony

No music theory needed. If you can hum it, Alchemy can work out what goes with it.

  1. Sing, hum or play

    Tap record and perform your idea. Your pitch draws live as you go, from your voice or any instrument.

  2. Find your key

    Alchemy picks out every note and finds the key your melody fits, in all seven modes. It listens like a singer does: which notes you sing and hold, and where your phrases come to rest.

  3. Choose your chords

    Get a progression built around your melody, on its own beat and bars. Tap any chord to hear what else would work.

Made for songwriters

Everything you need to catch an idea before it slips away, then grow it into a song.

What's inside

Careful listening and real harmony, in an app that stays out of your way.

Accurate note detection

Follows your notes through vibrato, slides and drifting pitch, and hears quiet singing too.

All seven modes

Major and minor, and Dorian, Mixolydian, Lydian, Phrygian and Locrian when your melody asks for them, with how likely each key is.

Your harmony

Chords that fit your melody, changing every two bars or as often as every beat, on bar lines you can see and move.

Layers and count-in

Record a harmony, an instrument or a beat over your take, with a metronome, count-in and loop recording.

Bring in Voice Memos

Share a voice memo or any audio file to Alchemy and find its key and chords.

Your library

Save every idea, as many as you like, and share any chord chart as text.

Sounds and styles

Private by design
