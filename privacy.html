Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

Alchemy for iPhone does not collect any of your data, store it on a server, or share it. Everything you record stays on your device.

Microphone

Alchemy uses the microphone only while you are recording, so it can find the notes, key and chords of what you sing or play. The audio is analysed on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

Your recordings and library

Takes you save are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete them in the Library or remove the app. They are shared only when you choose to share a chord chart or MIDI file through the iOS share sheet.

Files you send to Alchemy

Audio you share to Alchemy from other apps, such as Voice Memos, is copied into the app and analysed on your device. It is never uploaded.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites. This website does not use cookies or analytics either.

Children

Because Alchemy collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: mediavanity@gmail.com.
