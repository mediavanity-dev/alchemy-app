Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email mediavanity@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were recording. Alchemy needs iOS 16 or later.

Alchemy doesn't hear me

Check that Alchemy has microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. For quiet singing, hold your iPhone about 30 cm (a foot) away, and record away from loud background noise.

How do I import a Voice Memo?

In Voice Memos, tap the recording, tap ⋯, then Share, and choose Alchemy. If Alchemy isn't listed, scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More and add it. You can also open any audio file from Files with Import audio on the Capture screen.

The key isn't what I expected

Alchemy shows the likeliest keys in a row under the title, with how likely each one is; tap another to use it. It judges the key from the notes you sing and hold, and above all the note you end on, so a phrase that stops before it comes home can point somewhere else. Try singing the idea through to where it feels finished.

The chords don't sound right

Try another key from the row of keys, or tap any chord to choose an alternative. Chords can change every sets how often the chord may change: every 2 bars, every bar, every half bar or on any beat. With Alchemy Pro, Progressions offers other progressions in the same key.

The bar lines are in the wrong place

Press and hold the tempo on the Harmony screen. There you can set the tempo, the time signature and which beat your first note falls on. Recording with the count-in gives exact bars from the start.

How do I get my song into GarageBand or another DAW?

With Alchemy Pro, tap the share button on the Harmony screen, then MIDI file. You get separate melody, chords and bass tracks, laid out on the beat.

What does Alchemy Pro include?

More progressions in each key, extended chords, four more chord sounds and three more playing styles, up to 6 layers, Tune and lock to beat, and MIDI export. It's a one-time purchase, shared with your family through Family Sharing. Tap Upgrade at the top of the app to see it.

How do I get Pro back on a new iPhone?

Pro comes back by itself when you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. If it doesn't, tap Upgrade, then Restore purchases.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone. Alchemy has no accounts and sends nothing anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your saved takes, so export anything you want to keep first. See the privacy policy.
